Shaddoll Deck 🌑

  • Main gameplan: Use Shaddoll monsters and Fusion Summoning to control the field while triggering powerful effects when Shaddoll cards are sent to the Graveyard.
  • Strengths: Strong Fusion plays, excellent resource recursion, disruptive flip effects, and powerful boss monsters such as El Shaddoll Construct and El Shaddoll Winda.
  • Weaknesses: Can be vulnerable to graveyard hate, disruption at key points in the Fusion process, and decks that can consistently break established Shaddoll boards.
  • Playstyle: Control-oriented and resource-focused, using Fusion Monsters and Graveyard effects to generate advantage while disrupting the opponent's plays.
  • Below are several dropdowns: the first provides the decklist, while the others showcase different Shaddoll combo lines, Fusion plays, and possible end boards.

Lev Shaddoll Fusion
🔽

  • Activate Lev Shaddoll Fusion and send Shaddoll Apkallone to the Graveyard.
  • Activate the Graveyard effect of Shaddoll Apkallone to add Shaddoll Squamata to hand, then discard Shaddoll Squamata .
  • Activate the Graveyard effect of Shaddoll Squamata to send Tohushaddoll Grysta from the Deck to the Graveyard.
  • Activate the Graveyard effect of Tohushaddoll Grysta to Fusion Summon El Shaddoll Meshachrer using Shaddoll Apkallone , Shaddoll Squamata , and Tohushaddoll Grysta .
  • Activate El Shaddoll Meshachrer to add Void Unleashing to hand.
  • Activate Lev Shaddoll Fusion to Fusion Summon El Shaddoll Construct by using El Shaddoll Meshachrer as material.
  • Activate El Shaddoll Construct to send Shaddoll Hedgehog from the Deck to the Graveyard.
  • Activate the Graveyard effect of Shaddoll Hedgehog to add Reeshaddoll Wendi to hand.
  • Activate Void Unleashing , reveal Reeshaddoll Wendi , then add El Shaddoll Fusion and El Shaddoll Schism to hand, then discard a random card.
  • Activate El Shaddoll Fusion to Fusion Summon El Shaddoll Wendikurhu using El Shaddoll Construct and Reeshaddoll Wendi .
  • Activate the Graveyard effects of Reeshaddoll Wendi and El Shaddoll Construct to add El Shaddoll Fusion to hand and Set Naelshaddoll Ariel from the Deck.
  • Activate El Shaddoll Wendikurhu to flip Naelshaddoll Ariel face-up without changing its battle position.
  • Activate Naelshaddoll Ariel to Set Shaddoll Squamata from the banished zone.
  • Set El Shaddoll Schism .
  • Set El Shaddoll Fusion .

    End Board

  • 🌀 El Shaddoll Wendikurhu can flip Naelshaddoll Ariel face-down for another interaction.
  • ⛔ Naelshaddoll Ariel can send Tohushaddoll Grysta to the Graveyard to Fusion Summon El Shaddoll Shekhinaga , providing an Omni-Negate.
  • 🚫 El Shaddoll Fusion can Fusion Summon El Shaddoll Winda , limiting Special Summons and disrupting the opponent's turn.
  • 💥 El Shaddoll Schism can Fusion Summon during the opponent's turn by sending Elder Entity N'tss to the Graveyard, destroying a card while also enabling Shaddoll Apkallone to provide another negate.
  • Total of multiple layered disruptions, with El Shaddoll Winda summoned as soon as possible to restrict Special Summons.
  • 🎯 Use El Shaddoll Schism strategically to negate the opponent's most important card.
