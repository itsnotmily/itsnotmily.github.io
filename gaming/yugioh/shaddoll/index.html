Shaddoll Deck 🧵🖤

  • Main gameplan: Use Lev Shaddoll Fusion to efficiently load the GY, chain Shaddoll effects together, establish multiple Fusion monsters, and finish on Winda with Schism ready to disrupt the opponent.
  • Strengths: Strong GY setup, multiple Fusion lines, flexible disruption, and excellent resource recursion.
  • Weaknesses: Can be vulnerable if key GY effects or Fusion effects are negated before the engine gets established.
  • Playstyle: Establish a Fusion-based control board while preparing Shaddoll Schism for an opponent-turn Fusion.
  • Key priority: Summon Winda as soon as possible and use Schism to answer specific cards rather than activating it blindly.
Disruption Usage
🔽

  • ⛔ Dracotail Flame : Negate the activation of a Spell Card. After resolution, shuffle one card from your Graveyard into the Deck, then draw one card.
  • ↩️ Dracotail Horn : Return one Attack Position monster your opponent controls to the hand, then draw one card.
  • 💥 Dracotail Gulamel : Must be chained to the activation of a Dracotail card. Destroy one card on the field.
  • 🔀 Dracotail Faimena : Enables a Fusion Summon during the opponents turn, converting into an additional disruption.

Fusion Disruption Options

  • 🔀 Dracotail Filia : Banish one card on the field. Strong against decks reliant on key face-up cards.
  • ↩️ Dracotail Arthalion : Return cards to the hand equal to the number of Fusion Materials used from your hand.
  • 🚫 Secretarian Dragon : Applies a floodgate effect against Dragon and Spellcaster monsters. Only choose this when you know your opponent’s Deck.

Practical Notes

  • 🔀 Dracotail Faimena should fuse using one monster on the field and at least one monster from your hand to maximize value.
  • ⛔ ↩️ Dracotail Flame and Dracotail Horn both draw cards, often leading into additional Dracotail follow-up.
  • 🔀 Using a Dracotail monster as Fusion Material enables further disruption on the following turn.
