Dracotail Deck 🐉🔥🌪️

  • Main gameplan: Swarm Dragon monsters to enable Synchro and Xyz plays while maintaining pressure through recursion and extension.
  • Strengths: High combo potential, strong follow-up turns, and access to powerful Dragon-based bosses.
  • Weaknesses: Can struggle against heavy interruption and may require precise sequencing to avoid overextension.
  • Playstyle: Aggressive and adaptive, capable of explosive turns while still setting up solid boards.
  • Below are several dropdowns: the first provides the decklist, and the others showcase different combo lines and end boards.

Lukias or Ketu + 1
🔽

  • Normal Summon Dracotail Lukias .
  • Activate Dracotail Lukias to add Dracotail Faimena .
  • Activate Dracotail Faimena in hand.
  • Fusion Summon Dracotail Arthalion using Dracotail Lukias and one monster from hand.
  • Activate Dracotail Arthalion , target Dracotail Faimena , chain Dracotail Lukias .
  • Set Dracotail Rahu from the Deck and add Dracotail Faimena to hand.
  • Activate Dracotail Rahu .
  • Special Summon Dracotail Gulamel using Dracotail Faimena and Dracotail Urgula .
  • Activate Dracotail Faimena and Dracotail Urgula to set Dracotail Horn and Dracotail Flame .
  • Graveyard effect of Dracotail Urgula to add Dracotail Faimena to hand.

End Board

  • 💥 Dracotail Gulamel can destroy one card.
  • ↩️ Dracotail Horn returns one monster to the hand and draws one card.
  • ⛔ Dracotail Flame negates a Spell Card and draws one card.
  • 🔀 Dracotail Faimena can Fusion Summon into an additional disruption.
  • Total of four disruptions.
