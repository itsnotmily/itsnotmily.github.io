Dracotail Deck 🐉🔥🌪️

  • Main gameplan: Swarm Dragon monsters to enable Synchro and Xyz plays while maintaining pressure through recursion and extension.
  • Strengths: High combo potential, strong follow-up turns, and access to powerful Dragon-based bosses.
  • Weaknesses: Can struggle against heavy interruption and may require precise sequencing to avoid overextension.
  • Playstyle: Aggressive and adaptive, capable of explosive turns while still setting up solid boards.
  • Below are several dropdowns: the first provides the decklist, and the others showcase different combo lines and end boards.

Disruption Usage
🔽

  • [Dracotail Flame]: Negates the activation of a Spell Card. After resolution, shuffles one card from your Graveyard into the Deck, then draws one card.
  • [Dracotail Horn]: Returns one Attack Position monster your opponent controls to the hand, then draws one card.
  • [Dracotail Gulamel]: Must be chained to the activation of a Dracotail card to destroy one card on the field.
  • [Dracotail Faimena]: Enables a Fusion Summon during the opponent's turn, converting into an additional disruption.

    • Fusion Disruption Options

    • [Dracotail Filia]: Banishes one card on the field. Strong against decks reliant on key face-up cards.
    • [Dracotail Arthalion]: Returns cards to the hand equal to the number of Fusion Materials used from your hand.
    • [Secretarian Dragon]: Applies a floodgate effect against Dragon and Spellcaster monsters. Only choose this when you know your opponent's Deck.

    Practical Notes

    • [Dracotail Faimena]: Should fuse using one monster on the field and at least one monster from your hand to maximize value.
    • [Dracotail Flame] and [Dracotail Horn]: Both draw cards, often leading into additional Dracotail follow-up.
    • 🔀 Using a Dracotail monster as Fusion Material enables further disruption on the following turn.
Lukias or Ketu + 1
🔽

  • Normal Summon: [Dracotail Lukias]: Add [Dracotail Faimena].
  • [Dracotail Faimena]: Fusion Summon [Dracotail Arthalion] using [Dracotail Lukias] and one monster from hand.
  • [Dracotail Arthalion]: Target [Dracotail Faimena], then chain [Dracotail Lukias].
  • [Dracotail Lukias]: Set [Dracotail Rahu] from the Deck while [Dracotail Arthalion] adds [Dracotail Faimena] to hand.
  • [Dracotail Rahu]: Fusion Summon [Dracotail Gulamel] using [Dracotail Faimena] and [Dracotail Urgula].
  • [Dracotail Faimena](1) and [Dracotail Urgula](2): Activate to set [Dracotail Horn] and [Dracotail Flame].
  • GY [Dracotail Urgula]: Add [Dracotail Faimena] to hand.

    • End Board

    • [Dracotail Gulamel]: Can destroy one card.
    • [Dracotail Horn]: Returns one monster to the hand and draws one card.
    • [Dracotail Flame]: Negates a Spell Card and draws one card.
    • [Dracotail Faimena]: Can Fusion Summon into an additional disruption.
    • Total of four disruptions.

Branded Fusion Combo
🔽

  • [Branded Fusion]: Fusion Summon [Albion the Branded Dragon] by sending [Fallen of Albaz] and [Dracotail Phryxul] to the Graveyard.
  • [Dracotail Phryxul]: Set [Dracotail Ketu] from Deck.
  • [Dracotail Ketu]: Add [Dracotail Lukias].
  • Normal Summon: [Dracotail Lukias]: Add [Dracotail Faimena].
  • [Dracotail Faimena]: Fusion Summon [Secretarion Dragon] using both monsters on the field, triggering [Dracotail Lukias] to set [Dracotail Rahu].
  • [Secretarion Dragon]: Special Summon [Dracotail Phryxul], Shuffle [Fallen of Albaz].
  • [Dracotail Phryxul]: Special Summon [Dracotail Faimena] then return it to hand.
  • [Dracotail Rahu]: Fusion Summon [Dracotail Gulamel] using [Dracotail Urgula] and [Dracotail Faimena].
  • [Dracotail Faimena](1): Set [Dracotail Flame], [Dracotail Urgula](2) sets [Dracotail Horn].
  • GY [Dracotail Urgula]: Add [Dracotail Faimena] to hand.
  • End your turn. Optionally add [Branded Fusion] for follow-up next turn.

    • End Board

  • [Dracotail Gulamel]: Can destroy one card.
  • [Dracotail Flame]: Negates a Spell Card.
  • [Dracotail Horn]: Returns one monster to hand and draws one card.
  • [Dracotail Faimena]: Can fuse into an additional disruption.
  • [Secretarian Dragon]: Applies a floodgate to all Dragons and Spellcasters your opponent controls.
  • Total of four disruptions plus a floodgate.
Lukias + Faimena + 1 Monster
🔽

  • Normal Summon: [Dracotail Lukias]: Search for [Dracotail Urgula]
  • [Dracotail Faimena]: Fusion Summon [Dracotail Arthalion] using [Dracotail Urgula] and one monster from your hand
  • [Dracotail Arthalion]: Target [Dracotail Faimena] and the second monster.
  • [Dracotail Urgula]: Chain. Sets [Dracotail Rahu] while [Dracotail Arthalion] adds both monsters to hand.
  • Link Summon: [Spirit with Eyes of Blue] using [Dracotail Lukias], then [Heavenly Spheres]: Link Summon using both monsters.
  • [Dracotail Rahu]: Fusion Summon [Dracotail Gulamel] by sending [Dracotail Phryxul] from Deck and [Dracotail Faimena] from hand
  • [Dracotail Arthalion]: Summons itself; [Dracotail Faimena] sets Flame; [Dracotail Phryxul] sets Horn.
  • GY [Dracotail Urgula]: Add [Dracotail Faimena] to hand.
  • End your turn.

End Board

  • [Dracotail Gulamel]: Destroys one card.
  • [Dracotail Flame]: Negates a Spell Card and draws one.
  • [Dracotail Horn]: Returns one monster to hand and draws one.
  • [Dracotail Faimena]: Fuses into an additional disruption.
  • [Heavenly Spheres]: Returns a card to hand and can Summon [Fallen of Albaz] into [Albion the Sanctifire Dragon], which can Summon [Spirit with Eyes of Blue] to lock your opponent out of Special Summoning.
  • Total of five disruptions plus a floodgate.
