Chimera Deck 🦁🐐🐉

  • Main gameplan: Utilize Chimera fusion cards for versatile monsters and efficient resource use.
  • Strengths: Flexible plays, resource recursion, and strong finishers.
  • Weaknesses: Relies on key combo pieces, vulnerable to negations and disruptions.
  • Playstyle: Versatile, can go first to control or second to break boards with powerful monsters.
  • Below are several dropdowns: the first provides the decklist, and the others showcase different combo paths.
